## About

I'm a full-stack engineer who likes owning the entire stack, from Spring Boot services and Postgres schemas up to React and Next.js interfaces. Lately my work lives at the intersection of AI and SaaS: building retrieval systems, auth and billing flows, and the unglamorous reliability work that gets them production-ready. Before going deeper on my own products, I spent around 4 years in industry, including owning the micro-frontend shell for an enterprise online banking platform.

I'm most interested in problems where good engineering and a real product instinct have to meet.
